# Meridian Goods — Refund & Return Policy (v2.3)

This policy is the **single source of truth** for all refund decisions. Rules are
evaluated by the automated policy engine; the support agent may explain and apply
them but may **never** override an engine decision.

## Eligibility rules

- **R1 — Return window.** Change-of-mind returns are accepted up to **30 days
  after the delivery date**. After 30 days, the order is not refundable.

- **R2 — Delivery required.** Orders that have shipped but not yet been
  delivered cannot be refunded as returns. The customer must wait for delivery
  (or refuse delivery). Orders not yet shipped fall under R10.

- **R3 — Damaged, defective, or incorrect items.** If an item arrives damaged,
  defective, or is not the item ordered, the customer must report it within
  **7 days of delivery**. Approved claims receive a **full refund including
  original shipping fees**. Reports after 7 days are not eligible under R3.

- **R4 — Non-returnable categories.** Items marked **final sale** and items in
  the **perishable** category are not refundable under any change-of-mind
  circumstance. (Defective claims under R3 still apply.)

- **R5 — Gift cards.** Gift cards are non-refundable and cannot be exchanged
  for cash.

- **R6 — Opened electronics.** Electronics returned for change-of-mind reasons
  incur a **15% restocking fee if opened**. Electronics that have been **used**
  (beyond inspection) are not eligible for change-of-mind return.

- **R7 — One refund per item.** An order line that has already been refunded
  cannot be refunded again.

- **R8 — Auto-approval limit.** Refunds up to **$400.00** may be approved
  automatically. Refunds **above $400.00** always require human review; the
  agent must create an escalation ticket (response within 1 business day).

- **R9 — Flagged accounts.** Accounts flagged for refund abuse require human
  review for **any** refund, regardless of amount. The agent must escalate.

- **R10 — Pre-shipment cancellation.** Orders that have not yet shipped may be
  cancelled for a **full refund including shipping fees**.

- **R11 — Item not received.** If tracking shows *delivered* but the customer
  reports the item was not received, the claim requires a **carrier
  investigation** (human review). Do not auto-refund.

## Process rules

- **R12 — Verification & consent.**
  1. The agent must verify identity with the **account email plus order ID**
     before disclosing order details or processing anything.
  2. The agent must run a policy eligibility check before making any promise.
  3. The agent must obtain the customer's **explicit confirmation** before
     processing an approved refund.
  4. Approved refunds are issued to the **original payment method within 5–10
     business days**. Shipping fees are refunded only under R3 and R10.
